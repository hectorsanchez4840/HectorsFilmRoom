About

Every great story starts with a moment. For me, it was realizing I didn't just want to watch them — I wanted to build them.

I'm Hector Sanchez, a creative producer, director, and storyteller out of Chicago. I make docs, branded content, and digital-first stories that are grounded in real people and real moments — with a cinematic touch that makes them hit different.

Currently sharpening my craft at Loyola University Chicago, earning my Master's in Digital Media and Storytelling, and running my own creative venture Hector's Film Room. My work lives at the intersection of culture, character, and camera.

I'm building toward a career behind the lens — features, documentaries, and digital-first series that give people more than just a highlight. They get the whole story.

Director's Statement

I make films because some stories don't get told — not because they aren't worth telling, but because nobody showed up with a camera.

My work is rooted in three things: the athlete nobody wrote about, the culture that's shifting before anyone notices, and the person whose name never makes the headline. Sports gave me my first language for storytelling — the discipline, the sacrifice, the humanity behind the highlight reel. But culture taught me that those same stories exist everywhere, in every block, every community, every generation trying to hold onto something real.

I don't just document. I listen first. Then I point the camera at what I heard.
